# 03 — Landing Page Copywriting & Architecture

## Hero Section
- **Badge:** Smart Agri-Tech for Morocco's Olive Value Chain
- **Headline:** Faster, Smarter Olive Harvesting and Handling—Gentle on Every Fruit
- **Subheadline:** Built to overcome seasonal labor shortages, heat stress, and quality loss in Morocco, our Smart Olive Machines combine adaptive sensors with intuitive field telemetry.
- **Primary CTA:** Request a Field Demo
- **Secondary CTA:** Estimate Operational Impact

## Section 1: Field Challenges
- **Critical Seasonal Labor:** Narrow harvest windows and scarce skilled crews slow down peak-season operations.
- **Heat & Dust Resilience:** Demanding climate conditions require ruggedized cooling, filtration, and reliable uptime.
- **Fruit & Canopy Integrity:** Uncalibrated mechanical force bruises olives and damages branches, cutting oil quality and next-season yield.

## Section 2: How Smart Olive Machines Work (By Operational Profile)
- **Commercial Estates:** Adaptive canopy sensors, real-time fuel/uptime telemetry, and rapid operator onboarding.
- **Agricultural Cooperatives:** Fast cross-grove presets, batch traceability per member, and synchronized mill logistics.
- **Olive Mills:** Cleaner intake with fewer bruised drupes, scheduled harvest arrivals, and steadier line throughput.

## Section 3: Interactive Efficiency Simulator & Demo Request
- Interactive sliders for orchard size (hectares) and fleet size (units) estimating campaign hours saved and fruit damage reduction.
- Direct lead capture form accepting either email or Moroccan phone number (`+212`).
