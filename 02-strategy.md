# 02 — Go-to-Market & Positioning Strategy

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Primary ICP:** Commercial olive estates (40–300+ ha) and agricultural cooperatives in Fès-Meknès, Marrakech-Safi, and Béni Mellal-Khénifra seeking to cut harvest time and labor dependency.
- **Secondary ICP:** Modern olive oil mills (*maâsras*) looking for cleaner, undamaged fruit intake and predictable delivery schedules.

## 2. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
Combine **adaptive sensor-driven automation** with **rugged field telemetry** to harvest and handle olives faster, reduce fruit bruising and branch damage, and lower seasonal labor dependency across Moroccan orchards.

## 3. Core Positioning Statement
> For Moroccan olive growers, cooperatives, and modern mills facing seasonal labor shortages, rising operating costs, and strict oil-quality requirements, **Smart Olive Machines** deliver sensor-calibrated harvesting and operational telemetry that maximize yield, protect fruit integrity, and ensure harvest continuity.

## 4. Strategic Pillars
1. **Gentle Precision:** Adaptive vibration/comb calibration protects tree canopies and minimizes drupe bruising.
2. **Built for Moroccan Conditions:** Dust-sealed electronics, high-ambient thermal resilience, and simplified maintenance.
3. **Measurable Campaign ROI:** Transparent tracking of hectares covered, hours saved, and fruit loss reduction.
