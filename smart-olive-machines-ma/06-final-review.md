# 06 — Executive Final Review

## Project Summary
The Venture Team completed the end-to-end go-to-market package for **Smart Olive Machines** in **Morocco**, moving sequentially through market research, strategic positioning, conversion copywriting, single-page web prototyping, and a two-pass QA verification loop.

## Deliverables Included
- `01-research.md`: Moroccan olive industry pain points, competitive landscape, and modernization drivers.
- `02-strategy.md`: ICP segmentation, UVP, and core positioning statement.
- `03-copywriting.md`: Full landing page copy and section architecture.
- `04-prototype/index.html`: Responsive prototype with profile tabs, efficiency simulator, and validated demo form.
- `05-qa-report.md`: QA audit log and resolution of the contact form validation bug.
